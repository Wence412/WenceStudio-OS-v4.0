# Governance Charter

## Purpose
This charter governs permanent WenceStudio OS assets, including agents, prompts, workflows, products, SOPs, frameworks, research records, decisions, and client-delivery materials.

## Lifecycle
`DRAFT` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `APPROVED` → `ACTIVE` → `DEPRECATED` → `ARCHIVED`.

No asset may be presented as authoritative until it is `APPROVED`.

## Required controls
Every governed asset must have a stable ID, owner, version, status, purpose, dependencies, validation record, and change log.

## Action controls
- AI may research, classify, summarize, and draft within approved scope.
- Human review is required before external publication, client commitments, outbound communications, or production-system changes.
- Sensitive, regulated, or client-confidential data requires a documented data classification and approved handling path.
- Credentials, identities, permissions, and network access must not be expanded without owner approval.

## Decision rights
The WenceStudio founder or formally designated owner approves authoritative assets, risk-tier changes, external integrations, and deprecations.
