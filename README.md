# WenceStudio OS v4.0

WenceStudio OS v4.0 is the governed business operating system for WenceStudio by SmartDesign. It organizes knowledge, products, workflows, prompts, agents, SOPs, and decision records into a connected, version-controlled source of truth.

## Operating principle

Business before technology. Systems before isolated tasks. Knowledge before production. Human approval before consequential external action.

## Repository structure

- `00-governance/` . rules, lifecycle, versioning, and approval controls
- `01-executive-os/` through `12-archive-os/` . operating-system charters
- `13-master-registry/` . canonical asset index and controlled vocabularies
- `14-intelligence-layer/` . relationships, dependency mapping, and decision traceability
- `15-templates/` . reusable documentation templates
- `16-systems-exchange/` . reserved for the governed discovery and implementation layer
