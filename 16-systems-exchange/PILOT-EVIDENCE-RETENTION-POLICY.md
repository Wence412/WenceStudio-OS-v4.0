# Pilot Evidence Retention Policy

## Default for public-data pilots

- Retain approved pilot inputs and generated drafts for **90 days** after the pilot test date.
- Delete those inputs and drafts at the end of the retention period.
- Retain the redacted gate record, test result summary, and deletion confirmation for **24 months**.
- Do not retain credentials, personal data, confidential data, or raw execution logs in this repository.

## Exceptions

Any confidential, restricted, client, employee, or regulated-data pilot requires a system-specific retention decision before testing. This default does not authorize its storage.

## Owner

WenceStudio Governance reviews this policy when data classification, storage location, or pilot scope changes.
